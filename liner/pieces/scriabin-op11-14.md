# Overview

Twenty-four bars of compressed force. Scriabin writes [[15-8-meter|15/8]], grouped 3+2+3+2+3+2: three unequal five-eighth surges in every bar. The bass answers with octave blows while the upper chords keep leaning forward.

The opening material is heard twice, then pushed into higher registers and a final *fff*—as loud as the score can ask—before one held E-flat-minor chord.

source: Scriabin, 24 Preludes Op. 11, first edition (M. P. Belaieff, 1897); Knute Snortum edition, IMSLP #887472/#887473
