# Overview

A miniature ceremonial march from [[schumann|Schumann]]'s *Kinderszenen*—*Scenes from Childhood*. Heavy dotted rhythms, broad chords and sudden [[sforzando|sforzando]] accents make a child's “important event” sound magnificently official.

Its design is unusually clear: an eight-bar outer idea, a contrasting middle repeated twice, the outer idea again, and a one-bar close.

source: Schumann, *Kinderszenen*, Op. 15, first edition (Breitkopf & Härtel, 1839); Bernd Krüger MIDI edition
