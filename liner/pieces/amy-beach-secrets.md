# Overview

The secret is visible in the notation but easy to miss by ear. Two-note figures alternate between the hands; inside them, the first note of each pair is also written as a longer quarter note. Those held notes join into a melody concealed within the accompaniment.

[[amy-beach|Beach]] published *Children's Carnival* in 1894 as six short pieces for young players. This performance omits the written repeat and proceeds to the second ending, keeping the miniature near ninety seconds.

source: Beach, *Children's Carnival*, first edition (Arthur P. Schmidt, 1894); Beach autograph manuscript, Library of Congress A. P. Schmidt Company Archives
