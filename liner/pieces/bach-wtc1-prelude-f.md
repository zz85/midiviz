# Overview

The eleventh prelude of [[well-tempered-clavier|The Well-Tempered Clavier]], Book I. Fast broken-chord figures alternate with long decorated notes: one hand sustains and ornaments while the other keeps the current flowing.

The piece is only eighteen bars, but each 12/8 bar holds twelve eighth notes. It begins in F major, travels through closely related keys, and packs its strongest harmonic compression into the final bars.

source: Bach-Gesellschaft edition; Mutopia Project edition by Stelios Samelis, public domain
