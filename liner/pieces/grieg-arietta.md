# Overview

A small melody over continuously turning broken chords. [[grieg|Grieg]] begins with four repeated Gs, holds a low E-flat beneath the changing harmony, and lets the accompaniment provide motion while the tune barely raises its voice.

This is the first of Grieg's 66 *Lyric Pieces*. Its opening texture returns after a more active middle, but the final bars shorten and quiet the thought rather than simply repeating it.

source: Grieg, *Lyric Pieces*, Op. 12, first edition (Horneman, 1867); C. F. Peters edition, ed. Adolf Ruthardt, Sibley 1802/22702
