# Overview

A bright one-minute miniature from Tchaikovsky's *Children's Album*. The melody rises one step at a time over pairs of detached left-hand chords, swells suddenly, and starts again.

The published order makes this No. 9. Some editions follow the order of Tchaikovsky's autograph and print it as No. 6; that is why both numbers can be found.

source: Tchaikovsky Research, “Children's Album”; first edition (P. Jurgenson, 1878); G. Schirmer edition, ed. Adolf Ruthardt (1904)
