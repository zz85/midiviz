# Overview

A quick character piece built from two sharply different gestures: light chords high on the keyboard and a lower, more grounded reply. Grieg marks it *Molto allegro e sempre staccato*—very fast and always detached.

The opening eight-bar idea immediately repeats. A lower middle idea follows, then the two characters alternate before an eight-bar coda drives upward to the last chord.

source: Grieg, *Lyric Pieces*, Op. 12, first edition (Horneman & Erslev, 1867); Bernd Krüger MIDI edition
