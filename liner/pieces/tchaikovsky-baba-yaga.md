# Overview

A forty-five-bar chase in E minor, preceded by a three-eighth pickup. Tchaikovsky labels it “Baba-Yaga,” the supernatural figure of Slavic folklore; English editions variously call it *The Witch*, *The Old Witch* or *The Sorcerer*.

Its machinery is musical rather than pictorial: repeated [[sforzando|sforzando]] chords, quick [[staccato|staccato]] steps, an octave-higher return, and a final descent into the bass.

source: Tchaikovsky, *Children's Album*, first edition (P. Jurgenson, 1878); Tchaikovsky Research, “Children's Album”
