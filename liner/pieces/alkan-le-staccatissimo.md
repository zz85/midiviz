# Overview

The title intensifies [[staccato|staccato]] into a character: the most detached possible. Repeated notes glitter in the upper part while scales and broken figures run below, forcing the two hands to articulate different kinds of speed.

It is No. 2 of Alkan's 49 *Esquisses*—“sketches”—a collection that moves through every major and minor key before ending with an extra forty-ninth piece, *Laus Deo*.

source: Alkan, *Esquisses*, Op. 63, first edition (Richault, 1861); Hugh Macdonald, “Alkan,” *Grove Music Online*
