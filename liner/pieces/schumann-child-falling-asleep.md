# Overview

The penultimate scene in [[schumann|Schumann]]'s *Kinderszenen* does not imitate a lullaby so much as the act of losing consciousness. A one-bar rocking figure keeps returning, but its harmony, register and melodic focus gradually become harder to hold in place.

The quiet final phrase does not provide a firm E-minor cadence. It pauses on an unresolved A in the bass, allowing the last scene, *The Poet Speaks*, to follow like a reflection after sleep.

source: Schumann, *Kinderszenen*, first edition (Breitkopf & Härtel, 1839); Bärenreiter, notes to BA 9639

# Sections

## m1 Rocking figure
The opening idea is stated and partially repeated, always under long slurs.

## m9 Lower shadows
The bass drops and the texture grows darker while the rocking rhythm continues.

## m17 Distant middle
Four sharps replace one: the harmony and melodic line move into a more remote space.

## m25 Opening remembered
The first figure returns, shortened and altered.

## m32 Unfinished sleep
A final low A is left alone.

# Moments

Hear the upper notes as a melody floating over an accompaniment that never quite stops rocking.
