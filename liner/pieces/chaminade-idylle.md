# Overview

A melody marked *dolce* and *bien chanté*—sweetly, and well sung—floats over recurring three-note broken chords. [[chaminade|Chaminade]] changes pedal about twice per bar, refreshing the harmony while preserving the easy sway of 6/8.

The central span moves the accompaniment higher and broadens the register. A fermata and *poco ritardando* prepare the return, after which the opening texture leads to a smaller final slowing.
