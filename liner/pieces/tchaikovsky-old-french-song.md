# Overview

A bare, songlike melody in G minor, supported first by only a few bass notes and inner voices. Tchaikovsky states the opening strain twice, replaces its sparse accompaniment with flowing detached eighth notes, then brings the old song back.

The return strips away the middle section's flowing motion and brings the song quietly back to G minor. Tchaikovsky later reused the tune for the minstrels' song in Act II of *The Maid of Orleans*.

source: Tchaikovsky, *Children's Album*, first edition (P. Jurgenson, 1878); Tchaikovsky Research, “Children's Album,” related works
