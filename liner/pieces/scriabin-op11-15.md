# Overview

Eight bars pass before the right hand plays a note. Soft paired chords in the left hand establish a slow, breathing field; only then does a long melody enter above them. The middle reverses that disposition, placing quiet oscillating chords higher while another line is heard below.

This is No. 15 of Scriabin's 24 Preludes, a set that visits every major and minor key. Its D-flat warmth provides an immediate release after the violent E-flat-minor Prelude No. 14.

source: Scriabin, *24 Preludes*, first edition (M. P. Belaieff, 1897); Knute Snortum edition, IMSLP #887472/#887473
