---
title: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky
kind: composer
subtitle: 1840 Votkinsk – 1893 Saint Petersburg
---

Russian composer of symphonies, operas, ballets and piano music. He wrote the 24 pieces of the *Children's Album*, Op. 39, in 1878, describing the project as “a series of little individual pieces just for children, and with an attractive title, like Schumann's.”

source: Tchaikovsky Research, “Children's Album,” citing letters 820 and 823 (1878)
