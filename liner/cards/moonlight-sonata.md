---
title: Moonlight Sonata
kind: work
subtitle: Piano Sonata No. 14 in C♯ minor, Op. 27 No. 2 (1801)
---

Three movements, *Adagio sostenuto*, *Allegretto* and *Presto agitato*, published in 1802 as a *Sonata quasi una fantasia* and dedicated to Countess Giulietta Guicciardi. The order is unusual: a slow, quiet first movement, a short minuet-like Allegretto, and a stormy finale that carries the weight of the piece.

Beethoven asks for the first movement to be played very delicately with the dampers raised (*senza sordino*), and for the second to follow without a break. Franz Liszt is said to have called the Allegretto "a flower between two abysses".

The nickname comes from the critic Ludwig Rellstab, who in 1832 compared the first movement to moonlight on Lake Lucerne.

source: Charles Rosen, *Beethoven's Piano Sonatas: A Short Companion* (Yale, 2002); Wikipedia, "Piano Sonata No. 14 (Beethoven)"
