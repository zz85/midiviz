---
title: Fantaisie-Impromptu
kind: work
subtitle: Frédéric Chopin, Op. 66, C♯ minor (1834)
---

A fast outer section of rolling figures in both hands around a slow, singing middle section. Chopin kept it unpublished; after his death his friend Julian Fontana published it in 1855 as Op. 66.

In 1947 the theorist Ernst Oster argued that its opening follows the harmonies of the finale of Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata, in the same key.

source: Ernst Oster, "The Fantaisie-Impromptu: A Tribute to Beethoven", *Musicology* 1 (1947)
