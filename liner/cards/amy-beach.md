---
title: Amy Beach
kind: composer
subtitle: 1867 Henniker – 1944 New York City
---

American composer and pianist, born Amy Marcy Cheney. Her works include songs, chamber music, piano music and the *Gaelic Symphony*. Much of her music was published by the Boston firm Arthur P. Schmidt, whose archive—including Beach's manuscript of *Children's Carnival*—is held by the Library of Congress.

source: Library of Congress, “The Music of Amy Beach” and A. P. Schmidt Company Archives collection guide
