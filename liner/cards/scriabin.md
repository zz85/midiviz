---
title: Alexander Scriabin
kind: composer
subtitle: 1872 Moscow – 1915 Moscow
---

Russian composer and pianist. His early piano music grows from the Romantic prelude and étude, especially Chopin's models; his later music developed an increasingly individual harmonic language. The 24 Preludes, Op. 11, were written between 1888 and 1896 and traverse all 24 major and minor keys.

source: Bowers, *Scriabin: A Biography* (Dover, 1996); first edition of the 24 Preludes, M. P. Belaieff, 1897
