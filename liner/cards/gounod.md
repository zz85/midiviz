---
title: Charles Gounod
kind: composer
subtitle: 1818 Paris – 1893 Saint-Cloud
---

French composer, best known for the operas *Faust* (1859) and *Roméo et Juliette* (1867), and for church music: he once thought of becoming a priest. He won the Prix de Rome in 1839 and in Rome came to know the music of Palestrina, and, through Fanny Mendelssohn Hensel, of Bach.

His *Méditation* on Bach's first prelude (1853) became the Ave Maria, the best known of all his works.
