---
title: Franz Liszt
kind: composer
subtitle: 1811 Raiding – 1886 Bayreuth
---

Hungarian pianist, composer, conductor and teacher, central to the public image of the nineteenth-century virtuoso. Beside large concert works, he left many brief album leaves: music written into private albums as souvenirs, greetings or gifts.

source: Alan Walker, *Franz Liszt*, vol. 1 (Cornell University Press, 1983); Liszt work catalogue, S. 164 album leaves
