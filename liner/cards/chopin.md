---
title: Frédéric Chopin
kind: composer
subtitle: 1810 Żelazowa Wola – 1849 Paris
---

Polish composer and pianist who settled in Paris in 1831 and wrote almost only for the piano: nocturnes, études, preludes, mazurkas, polonaises, ballades. He and Schumann were born a few months apart in 1810, and Schumann's first published article was a review that introduced Chopin to German readers.

source: Kornel Michałowski and Jim Samson, "Chopin, Fryderyk Franciszek," *Grove Music Online*
