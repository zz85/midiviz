---
title: Edvard Grieg
kind: composer
subtitle: 1843 Bergen – 1907 Bergen
---

Norwegian composer and pianist whose music joined European Romantic forms to Norwegian dance rhythms and melodic turns. His ten books of *Lyric Pieces* contain 66 piano miniatures written across more than three decades; *Elves' Dance* is in the first book.

source: John Horton and Nils Grinde, “Grieg, Edvard,” *Grove Music Online*; Edvard Grieg Museum Troldhaugen, biography
