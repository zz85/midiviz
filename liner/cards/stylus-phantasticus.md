---
title: Stylus phantasticus
kind: term
subtitle: the "fantastic style"
---
A free, improvising manner of 17th- and early 18th-century instrumental music, grown from Italian organ toccatas and fantasias and carried north by Frescobaldi's pupil Froberger. Athanasius Kircher called it in 1650 the most free and unrestrained way of composing, bound neither to words nor to a melodic subject.

Mattheson described the free sections of Buxtehude's preludes in these terms: now swift, now hesitating, now behind the beat, without measure, to astonish.

source: Athanasius Kircher, *Musurgia universalis* (Rome, 1650); Johann Mattheson, *Der vollkommene Capellmeister* (Hamburg, 1739); Paul Collins, *The Stylus Phantasticus and Free Keyboard Music of the North German Baroque* (2005)
