---
title: Ludwig van Beethoven
kind: composer
subtitle: 1770 Bonn – 1827 Vienna
---

Born in Bonn and baptised on 17 December 1770, he moved to Vienna in 1792 to study with Haydn and stayed there for the rest of his life, first as a virtuoso pianist and then as the most famous composer in Europe. He wrote 32 piano sonatas, 9 symphonies, 16 string quartets and one opera, *Fidelio*.

His hearing began to fail in his late twenties. By 1818 he could no longer follow a conversation and used notebooks; many of his greatest works, the Ninth Symphony and the late quartets among them, were written when he was almost entirely deaf.

source: Lewis Lockwood, *Beethoven: The Music and the Life* (Norton, 2003)
