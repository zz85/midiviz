---
title: Anatoly Lyadov
kind: composer
subtitle: 1855 Saint Petersburg – 1914 Polïnovka
---

Russian composer, pianist and teacher associated with the circle around Rimsky-Korsakov and Belyayev. He wrote with unusual concentration, favoring songs, piano miniatures and short orchestral works over large forms. The B-minor Prelude is the first of his Three Pieces, Op. 11.

source: Jennifer Spencer, “Lyadov, Anatoly Konstantinovich,” *Grove Music Online*; Encyclopaedia Britannica, “Anatoly Lyadov”
