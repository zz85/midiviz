---
title: Carnaval, Op. 9
kind: work
subtitle: Robert Schumann, 1834–35
---

Twenty-one short piano pieces subtitled *Scènes mignonnes sur quatre notes*, "little scenes on four notes": a masked ball where commedia dell'arte figures, Schumann's friends and Schumann's own two selves pass by in turn. Almost every piece grows from the letters A, S, C, H, read as notes in German names.

source: Robert Schumann, *Carnaval*, Op. 9, first edition (Breitkopf & Härtel, 1837); John Daverio and Eric Sams, "Schumann, Robert," *Grove Music Online*
