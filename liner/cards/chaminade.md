---
title: Cécile Chaminade
kind: composer
subtitle: 1857 Paris – 1944 Monte-Carlo
---

French composer and pianist whose catalogue includes orchestral music, songs and more than a hundred piano pieces. Her shorter works circulated widely in the early twentieth century. *Idylle* opens the second series of her *Album des enfants*, Op. 126, published in 1907.

source: Marcia J. Citron, “Chaminade, Cécile,” *Grove Music Online*; Enoch first edition of Op. 126 (1907)
