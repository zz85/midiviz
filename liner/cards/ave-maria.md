---
title: Ave Maria (Bach–Gounod)
kind: work
subtitle: Charles Gounod, 1853 / 1859
---

Charles Gounod (1818–1893) improvised a melody over this prelude and published it in 1853 as *Méditation sur le 1er prélude de piano de S. Bach*. In 1859 the Latin text of the Ave Maria was set to it, and the song became a favourite of singers, violinists and church weddings.

The piano part is Bach's prelude almost unchanged. Gounod played from an edition with the extra bar after bar 22 (the Schwencke bar), so the song keeps it.
