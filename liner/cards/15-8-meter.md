---
title: 15/8 meter
kind: term
---

A bar containing fifteen eighth notes. Scriabin does not treat them as fifteen equal pulses in this prelude: the score groups them 3+2+3+2+3+2, so three longer five-eighth spans drive the bar forward.

source: Scriabin, Prelude Op. 11 No. 14, first edition (Belaieff, 1897)
