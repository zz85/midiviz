---
title: The Well-Tempered Clavier
kind: work
subtitle: Das Wohltemperirte Clavier, BWV 846–893
---

Two books of 24 preludes and fugues each, one pair in every major and minor key, in rising chromatic order. Bach finished Book I in 1722 and compiled Book II around 1740–42, so the set is often called "the 48".

Earlier composers had written in many keys, but Bach's set made the case that a well-tuned keyboard could play in all of them. Later cycles through every key include Chopin's 24 Preludes Op. 28 (in circle-of-fifths order; the Raindrop Prelude is No. 15) and Shostakovich's 24 Preludes and Fugues Op. 87.

The conductor Hans von Bülow is said to have called it the Old Testament of keyboard music, and Beethoven's 32 sonatas the New.

source: David Ledbetter, *Bach's Well-tempered Clavier: The 48 Preludes and Fugues* (Yale, 2002)
