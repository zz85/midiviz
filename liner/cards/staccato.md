---
title: Staccato
kind: term
---

Notes played distinctly separated rather than joined. A dot is the ordinary sign; a wedge can ask for *staccatissimo*, an especially detached attack. The exact shortness depends on tempo, instrument and musical context—it does not mean that every marked note has one fixed duration.

source: Elaine Gould, *Behind Bars* (Faber Music, 2011), “Staccato and staccatissimo”
