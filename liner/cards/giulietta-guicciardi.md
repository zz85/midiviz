---
title: Giulietta Guicciardi
kind: person
subtitle: Countess, 1782–1856
---

A young countess who took piano lessons from Beethoven in Vienna from about 1801, and the dedicatee of the Moonlight Sonata. In a letter of November 1801 Beethoven wrote of a girl he loved and who loved him, usually taken to be her.

In 1803 she married Count Wenzel Robert von Gallenberg, a composer of ballet music, and moved with him to Naples. Beethoven's first biographer, Anton Schindler, believed she was the "Immortal Beloved" of Beethoven's famous 1812 letter; most scholars now think that was someone else.

source: Lewis Lockwood, *Beethoven: The Music and the Life* (Norton, 2003); Maynard Solomon, *Beethoven* (2nd ed., Schirmer, 1998)
