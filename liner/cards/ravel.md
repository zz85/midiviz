---
title: Maurice Ravel
kind: composer
subtitle: 1875 Ciboure – 1937 Paris
---

French composer and pianist known for exacting orchestral colour and equally precise piano writing. He studied at the Paris Conservatoire with Gabriel Fauré. The tiny Prelude M. 65 was written for the same institution as a sight-reading examination rather than as part of a concert cycle.

source: Barbara L. Kelly, “Ravel, Maurice,” *Grove Music Online*; Bibliothèque nationale de France authority record for the Prelude
