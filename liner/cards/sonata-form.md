---
title: Sonata form
kind: term
---

The usual plan of a first movement (and often a finale) from about 1750: an **exposition** with a first theme in the home key and a second theme in another key, usually repeated; a **development** that takes the themes through other keys; a **recapitulation** that brings both themes back in the home key; and often a **coda**.

The drama is in the keys: the exposition sets up a conflict between two of them, and the recapitulation resolves it by keeping everything at home.
