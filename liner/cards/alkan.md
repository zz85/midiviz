---
title: Charles-Valentin Alkan
kind: composer
subtitle: 1813 Paris – 1888 Paris
---

French pianist-composer and a child prodigy at the Paris Conservatoire. He wrote almost entirely for keyboard, ranging from very large virtuoso structures to the 49 concentrated character pieces of *Esquisses*, Op. 63. *Le Staccatissimo* is the second of those sketches.

source: Hugh Macdonald, “Alkan, Charles-Valentin,” *Grove Music Online*; first edition of *Esquisses*, Richault, 1861
