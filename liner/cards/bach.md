---
title: Johann Sebastian Bach
kind: composer
subtitle: 1685 Eisenach – 1750 Leipzig
---

Organist, court musician and, from 1723 until his death, Thomaskantor in Leipzig: in charge of the music at the city's main churches. He wrote Book I of The Well-Tempered Clavier at Köthen, where he led the prince's court musicians from 1717 to 1723.

Over 1,000 of his works are numbered in the BWV (*Bach-Werke-Verzeichnis*, Wolfgang Schmieder, 1950). This prelude is BWV 846.

After his death his music was known mostly to keyboard players and composers. Felix Mendelssohn's 1829 performance of the St Matthew Passion in Berlin started the wider revival.

source: Christoph Wolff, *Johann Sebastian Bach: The Learned Musician* (Norton, 2000)
