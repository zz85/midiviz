---
title: Robert Schumann
kind: composer
subtitle: 1810 Zwickau – 1856 Endenich
---

German Romantic composer, pianist and critic. His piano cycles often bind small character pieces into a larger imaginative world: *Kinderszenen* looks back at childhood in thirteen miniatures, while the later *Album for the Young* was written for young players themselves.

source: John Daverio and Eric Sams, “Schumann, Robert,” *Grove Music Online*
