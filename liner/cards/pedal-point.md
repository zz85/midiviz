---
title: Pedal point
kind: term
---

A note held (or repeated) in the bass while the harmony above it changes, including chords it does not belong to. The name comes from the organ, where the player can hold a low note with a foot on the pedalboard.

A pedal on the dominant (G in C major) builds expectation for the tonic. A pedal on the tonic near the end of a piece says that the music has arrived home. Bach's Prelude in C has both: G from bar 24, C from bar 32. The finale of Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata waits on G♯ for thirteen bars before its recapitulation.
