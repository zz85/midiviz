**Bach: Goldberg Variations and the Well-Tempered Clavier (marked \*):** typeset by the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org) (Hajo Dezelski, Stelios Samelis, Davide Castellone, JD Erickson, Sven Reichard, Andrew Hawryluk and others), each piece CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0 or public domain. Joined here into one file per prelude and fugue (the Goldberg as the Aria, the 30 variations and the Aria again, 2.5 s apart). These are engraved scores, not performances, so they were given dynamics by rule (not by a pianist): each phrase swells and relaxes and the last one tapers, the music gets louder where it climbs and thickens, the top line leads and inner voices recede, long notes carry a little more weight, plus a small seeded variation. Timing is untouched. The Goldberg is then voiced from the score's actual staff and voice identities by [`tools/bach_goldberg_voicing.mjs`](tools/bach_goldberg_voicing.mjs): the Aria's ornamented melody leads; both voices of each canon project above its independent bass; Variation 10's fughetta subject entries and Variation 30's two song lines come forward; lyrical variations 7, 13, 19 and 25 sing above quieter accompaniment. The adjustments are modest (principal lines +4 to +10 MIDI velocity units, accompaniment -2 to -4), preserve the underlying phrase dynamics, and do not clip. Running `node midis/tools/bach_goldberg_voicing.mjs` starts from the fixed pre-ornament dynamics commit and then regenerates the ornaments, so both Goldberg files are deterministic. Ornaments are played: Mutopia's MIDI comes from LilyPond, which leaves out trills, mordents and turns and plays grace notes as 55 ms blips before the beat, so [tools/bach_ornaments.mjs](tools/bach_ornaments.mjs) reads the LilyPond sources and writes them out after Bach's own table of ornaments (1720): trills from the upper note at about 12 notes a second, short trills, mordents, turns, trills with turns and mordent endings, appoggiaturas on the beat taking half the note, grace notes on the beat. The Well-Tempered files gain 184 ornaments and 18 groups of grace notes, the Goldberg 185 and 113 (its Aria counted twice); the dynamics stay as they were. In the Well-Tempered files two more sources add signs: the Bach-Gesellschaft edition as encoded by CCARH ([humdrum-tools/bach-wtc](https://github.com/humdrum-tools/bach-wtc), 22 signs Mutopia lacks, 14 of them in the B minor prelude, plus the interval of every trill and mordent it marks), and the rule that a fugue subject's ornament is played on every entry (20 more, in the C major fugue of Book II and the B minor fugue of Book I). Last, a trill at the main cadences, where players add one though no edition prints it (C. P. E. Bach's rule: the note before a cadence's close): the final cadence of each prelude and fugue, and perfect authentic cadences on a downbeat away from Bach's own signs, on the note of the top line over the dominant that resolves to the tonic, 21 in all; `--no-cadences` leaves them out. How each is played, and how to rerun it, is at the top of the script.
